The backbone of handover

The ISBAR Framework

Structured clinical communication for patient safety — the exact flow the Report form follows.

IIdentify
SSituation
BBackground
AAssessment
RRecommend

I — Identification

Patient MRN, bed number, name, age, gender

S — Situation

Current problems and complaints

B — Background

Vital signs and clinical background

A — Assessment

Diagnosis and patient condition

R — Recommendation

Instructions, care plan, follow-up

The app's Report form mirrors this: IDENTIFICATION → BACKGROUND → SITUATION → ASSESSMENT → RECOMMENDATIONS, then NURSE INFORMATION. Consistent handovers reduce clinical errors and keep every shift aligned.

09

Hands-on reporting

Report Form

"Report — {department} — {shift} Shift" — pick a template, fill the sections, submit.

📝

Report Page

Workflow
  • Template cards: name, description, department, N fields → "Fill Out Form"
  • Attributed to you via the "Reporting As" chip (must be checked in)
  • Submit → "Submitted Successfully" → back to Dashboard
  • Documentation page = general forms without staff attribution
📋

Form Sections

Structure
  • IDENTIFICATION — MRN, BN (bed), Patient Name, Age, Gender
  • BACKGROUND — BP, Pulse, RR, Temp, SpO2, Pain (banded dropdowns)
  • SITUATION — current problems (multi-select)
  • ASSESSMENT — diagnoses · RECOMMENDATIONS — instructions
  • NURSE INFORMATION — name, Shift (Day/Night), Patient condition (Critical / Subcritical / Stable), NOTE!
⚡

Smart Features

Time Savers
  • MRN auto-populate — type an MRN (2+ chars) and patient name, age, gender, bed, DOB, allergies, diagnosis fill in
  • Required-field validation per section with error badges
  • Auto-calculated formula fields (read-only, purple label)
  • Handover forms: "Handover Locked" until shift staff are checked in

Data at your fingertips

All Records & Analytics

"ISBAR Records Database" — search every submission and explore hospital trends.

🗄️

All Records

Database
  • Search by MRN, patient name, template; smart syntax like last:7d
  • Filters: template, department, user, per-page (50–1000+)

Insights

  • Stats: Total Submissions · Critical Patients · Subcritical · Expired Drugs

  • Export Excel with Submissions, Stability, Resources, Departments sheets

All dates in the system use the Ethiopian calendar — analytics help department heads spot workload patterns and plan staffing and supplies accordingly.
