The ISBAR Framework

Structured clinical communication for patient safety

IIdentify
SSituation
BBackground
AAssessment
RRecommend

I — Identification

Patient name, MRN, bed, age, gender, your name & role

S — Situation

What's happening now? Symptoms, vitals, complaints

B — Background

Medical history, medications, allergies, procedures

A — Assessment

Clinical impression, stability status

R — Recommendation

Action plan, follow-up, escalation needs

Standardized communication reduces clinical errors by up to 30%. ISBAR ensures every handover contains critical information in a consistent format.
