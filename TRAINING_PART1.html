The ISBAR Framework

Structured clinical communication for patient safety

IIdentify
SSituation
BBackground
AAssessment
RRecommend
I — Identification
Patient name, MRN, bed number, age, gender, your name and role
S — Situation
What is happening now? Current complaints, symptoms, vital signs
B — Background
Medical history, current medications, allergies, recent procedures
A — Assessment
Clinical impression, stability status (Stable/Unstable/Critical)
R — Recommendation
Action plan, requested tasks, follow-up, escalation needs

Why ISBAR Matters

Standardized communication reduces clinical errors by up to 30%. ISBAR ensures every handover contains critical information in a consistent, scannable format.
